File an engineering issue for every support ticket into the engineering team's Triage (`LINEAR_TRIAGE_TEAM_ID`, optional) with the ticket as its sub-issue, so a request filed through the assistant reaches the engineering inbox without a manual hand-off, several tickets about one bug can be gathered under one issue, and closing the issue closes its tickets. The issue carries the whole ticket body under a link to the ticket; an issue that fails is reported, the support ticket stands.
