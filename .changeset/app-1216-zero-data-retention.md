Route every chat model call through providers under a zero-data-retention agreement (AI Gateway `zeroDataRetention`), so no provider keeps or trains on chat text; the deepseek first-party host drops out of routing. The documentation search's embedding and rerank calls, which carry the user's query, go only to providers that do not train on it (`disallowPromptTraining`): the voyage models have no zero-data-retention agreement on the gateway, so full zero data retention there needs other retrieval models. The model chain and the reasoning levels are now chosen with an eval checked in under `evals/` (`pnpm eval`): every kind of conversation the chat gets — documentation answers, capabilities the app lacks, facts it does not give, problem reports, feedback, off-topic, prompt injection, another language — run through the agent as the route wires it, checked against the prompt's rules and graded by a judge model. The chain becomes `openai/gpt-6-luna` at medium reasoning, then `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` at low, then `openai/gpt-6-sol` at low; each model's level is set in the config and applied through the provider-neutral AI SDK setting (medium lifts gpt-6-luna from 29 to 38 of 44 documentation conversations). `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` (13 of 44) leaves the chain. The LLM smoke test gains a documentation scenario: a capability the app may lack is answered, not filed.
