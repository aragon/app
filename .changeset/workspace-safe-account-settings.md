Serve Safe accounts on the workspace settings page. A Safe gets a read-only page: an Account section (name, avatar, chain and description), a Governance section (threshold linked to its members, nonce, guard and modules) and a Contracts aside (address, version and Safe app link, then the guard and each enabled module, named with its version when it is an official Safe module). The workspace navigation and the account selector now offer Settings for a Safe, and keep Proposals for DAO accounts only.
