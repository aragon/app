Serve the workspace proposals page under an account scope, `/workspace/{workspaceId}/{accountId}/proposals`, which the navigation already linked to and which answered with the in-workspace 404 of the section catch-all: the list, its process tabs and its aside card narrow to the selected account, a DAO account shows the same stats as the DAO proposals page, and "New proposal" skips the account step the route has already answered. Under an account scope the rows drop the tag naming their DAO and the tabs drop its name, both of which only repeated the page. Selecting a process now describes that process on the aside — the swap the DAO proposals page makes between the proposal stats and the process information — under either scope, since a process tab names one account whether or not the route does; the tabs moved into a `useWorkspaceProposalTabs` hook that the list and the page share, and which derives the selection from the URL alone rather than keeping a copy of it in state, so the card cannot describe a different tab than the one being shown. The aside also carries the Telegram notifications card of the DAO proposals page whenever exactly one DAO is in view — the account the route names, the one a selected process belongs to, or the only DAO of the workspace on its aggregated route — since a subscription is bound to a single DAO. A DAO holding a single process has no tab strip to select from, and its aside now describes that process rather than counting the DAO's proposals, which is what the DAO proposals page does in the same situation. A process with no proposals now says so as a filtered result ("No proposals found") rather than claiming the workspace has none. Three things the scope exposes are fixed with it: a selection holding no account rendered a skeleton forever instead of its empty state, because a disabled query reads as pending; the empty and error copy spoke of the accounts of the workspace whatever the scope, and now follows the route, so a workspace holding a single DAO still presents itself as the workspace on its aggregated route; and the scope is resolved from the route rather than from the account option, which is matched on the exact ID string, so a shared link carrying a lowercased address no longer widens the page to every account under a URL naming one. A Safe account scope still falls back to every DAO account, as the transactions page does, a Safe having no proposals of its own to show.
