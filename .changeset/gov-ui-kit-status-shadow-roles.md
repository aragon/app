The status shadow classes (`shadow-success-sm`, `shadow-info-md`, …) are semantic roles and follow the Next and Dark previews; their default values are unchanged. The status shadow primitives become `--guk-shadow-*` variables without classes of their own: `shadow-success`, `shadow-success-lg`, `-xl` and `-2xl` and their siblings are gone, and `shadow-success-default` is the role for `shadow-success`.
