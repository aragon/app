description: Use when someone types /aragon-app-canvas-update, or asks to bring the repo's latest prototype files onto their claude.ai design canvas of the Aragon App prototypes.
---

Follow **Update a canvas** in `design/RUNBOOK.md`, and only that section. Read `design/README.md` first if this
session has not.

The repo's files replace what is on the canvas. If the canvas has edits that are not in the repo, stop and offer
to save them first. No commit, no push.

The "Rules" and "Done when" of `skills/shared/aragon-app-canvas/SKILL.md` apply.
