# Inbox

Dumping ground for unstructured or incorrectly structured ideas — valid content that hasn't yet been shaped into proper wiki entries. Excluded from the wiki index (`wiki.toml` ignores `inbox/**`) but **committed to git**: unlike `raw/`, nothing here is regenerable.

Rules (the full convention lives in [WORKFLOW.md](../WORKFLOW.md)):

- **Everything in here is a draft.** Unindexed, unreviewed, possibly contradictory — never cite it as established fact.
- **Agents: do not read, reference, or process this folder unless the product owner explicitly asks.** Processing the inbox is its own requested task, not a side effect of other work.
- When asked to process: mine items into proper entries (filed by area, linked in, `status: draft` until validated), then delete the mined material from the inbox.
