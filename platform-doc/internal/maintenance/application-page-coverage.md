# Live application-page coverage

Release-scoped crosswalk produced by `audit-live-application-page-coverage`. It accounts for **17 live page families**: 15 explicit application routes and two plugin pages. Seven already had sufficient behavior coverage; ten received supported additions, including a new [Settings](../../accounts/settings.md) entry. Named sections make the wizard and plugin-page homes directly addressable. There are no outstanding placement or behavior-evidence dispositions at this audit's page-coverage depth.

The crosswalk is the input to `audit-consequential-drill-down-coverage` and `audit-page-purpose-and-repository-topology`. It records purpose, information, actions, scope and canonical homes; it does not certify every rendered value, deployed configuration, interaction method, or contract call. The completed [purpose and topology audit](./page-purpose-and-topology.md) updates these links: Rewards is now filed under Treasury, and shared application references under Application. The completed [section audit](./section-fit-and-location.md) preserves all 17 page-family anchors. Proposal details now separates [actions and execution](../../governance/proposal.md#actions-and-execution), [voting](../../governance/proposal.md#voting), and [description](../../governance/proposal.md#description-and-resources); Members owns [list order](../../governance/body.md#member-list-order); direct execution separates [eligibility](../../treasury/create-transaction.md#mechanics) from [composition and submission](../../treasury/create-transaction.md#composing-and-submitting). These are section-location changes using existing evidence, without a new route census or deployment claim.

## Census and consolidation

The [released route tree](https://github.com/aragon/app/tree/adad67873c8f9dd75e3ed340b70df3e985ae3557/apps/app/src/app) contains **18 `page.tsx` files**: 15 explicit live routes, two excluded policy routes, and one plugin catch-all. The catch-all resolves exactly two registered page families, Gauges and Rewards. Both their [registrations](https://github.com/aragon/app/blob/adad67873c8f9dd75e3ed340b70df3e985ae3557/apps/app/src/initPluginRegistry.ts) and plugin `index.ts` files were checked; only Gauge Voter and Capital Distributor register `APPLICATION_PLUGIN_PAGE` components or plugin `pageLinks` in this release. Thus 18 route files yield 17 live page families and two excluded route families, not 18 separate live pages.

[Account navigation](https://github.com/aragon/app/blob/adad67873c8f9dd75e3ed340b70df3e985ae3557/apps/app/src/modules/application/components/navigations/navigationDao/navigationDaoUtils.ts) supplies Dashboard, Proposals, Members, Assets, Transactions, Permissions and Settings, supplemented by the two installed-plugin links. The navigation dialog carries links absent from the desktop bar. Supported-plugin/body checks and [CMS visibility](../../application/app-cms.md) affect which links appear; they do not create another page family.

In the table, **Q** means `/dao/{network}/{addressOrEns}`; the bare Q route redirects to `Q/dashboard` in the release's `next.config.mjs`. Network and address/ENS variants share a home. Process/body/account selections, query parameters, proposal slugs, member addresses and wizard plugin addresses also remain variants of their page. Onboarding and bespoke dashboard content are states of Dashboard. Wallet/profile, action, claim, gauge-detail, installation and transaction dialogs stay with their capability; APIs, assets, layouts, error/not-found states and external destinations are not additional application pages. The common error treatment is already in [Collection pages](../../application/collection-pages.md#failures).

Names follow released navigation and wizard labels. Where a detail page's title is its object's name, the established **Member**, **Proposal details**, or **Process details** page name is used. The account-creation wizard's literal R1 label is **Create DAO**; its canonical **Create account page** heading follows the owner-approved [account terminology](../../accounts/account.md#account-versus-dao). The execution wizard is labelled **Execute transaction**, distinct from its entry button **Execution** and final action **Execute**.

## Addition evidence and close-out

The source links above establish the page callers. The additions also trace the following immediate R1 components, under `apps/app/src/`:

- **Settings:** `modules/settings/components/daoHierarchy`, `daoSettingsInfo`, `daoVersionInfo`, and the page's supported-process and OSx-version gates. Account metadata editing, Admin management and upgrades link to existing capability explanations rather than acquiring duplicate rules here.
- **Proposals and Process details:** `modules/governance/components/proposalListStats`, `modules/settings/components/daoPluginInfo`, and the process details page's read-only governance fields. The proposal details page supplies action JSON, creator and creation-transaction links.
- **Members and Member:** the Members page's selected-body aside, `modules/governance/components/delegationSection`, `delegationStatsCard`, and `memberDelegateButton`. The button distinguishes a new delegate from the connected holder's own/already-delegated member page. Existing profile/statement, token and avatar findings were reused.
- **Rewards:** `plugins/capitalDistributorPlugin/components/capitalDistributorCampaignList` and `capitalDistributorRewardsStats`, plus the server page's first-instance selection and country gate. The claimable row opens the claim dialog; the claimed row links to its recorded transaction. The overview does not turn the aside's “latest claim” label into a new chronological backend guarantee.
- **Gauges:** the page caller, `gaugeVoterGaugeDetailsDialog` and `gaugeVoterGaugeDetailsDialogContent`, reusing the R1 presentation findings already preserved in the classification audit. Gauge management remains separate from voting; BENQI registration fields do not become shared gauge-detail fields.
- **Names and entry points:** `assets/locales/en.json`, shared wizard layouts, the Settings Process action and Dashboard Permissions action. Existing `#mechanics`, `#creating-a-proposal` and `#self-serve-creation` anchors remain valid.

The new Settings entry is filed under Accounts because its distinct outcome is inspecting and entering changes to account configuration; it links to the capabilities that implement those changes. No contested ownership required a placement handoff. No material missing evidence required a new task. The completed topology audit retains Settings in Accounts and confirms these named page homes.

Proposal, Governance process, Body and Member return to draft for their added page behavior; their reviewed semantic definitions are preserved. Settings is a new draft. Previously draft pages keep that state; heading and navigation changes do not approve them. The backlog's draft inventory and the existing remaining-review task cover these additions. The audit retirement, validation result and board delta are recorded in [log.md](./log.md).
