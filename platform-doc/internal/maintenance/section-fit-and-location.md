## Stage and proposal structure recommendation

**2026-09-15 — accepted and implemented.** The owner requested three independent sub-agent arguments: retain Stage, Staged proposals, and Proposal; merge Staged proposals into Stage; or merge Staged proposals into Proposal. Each agent read the actual pages, their neighbors, taxonomy, and backlinks, then answered the strongest ownership objection to its option. The owner then instructed implementation of the Proposal merger and a narrative-flow sub-agent review of the modified pages. Stage and Proposal remain drafts; the retired Staged proposals content is reviewed within Proposal.

**Applied: merged Staged proposals into Proposal, retaining Stage as its own concept.** The deciding argument was continuity of the subject: Staged proposals explains the submitted proposal's progression, reports, next-stage activation, final execution, and presentation. Those are a specialized proposal lifecycle and experience. The **Staged proposals** section retains the coordination explanation and serves direct links from Process, the SPP catalogue, and governance patterns.

| Option | Strongest case | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep three, renaming Staged proposals to Staged governance | Coordination among bodies and stages is a useful subject: reporting, advancement, subsequent activation, and final execution need one coherent explanation. Existing readers arrive specifically for that subject. | A valid alternative, especially if the coordinated governance arrangement becomes the central subject of that page. The current content largely explains what happens to a proposal, so renaming alone does not establish enough independence from Proposal's lifecycle and Process's existing composition model. |
| Merge into Stage, possibly titled Stages | The rules and their consequences belong together: readiness, reports, progression, and stage presentation currently require repeated trips between two pages. | Causal completeness is valuable. However, the resulting stage concept would also own the consolidated proposal, transitions across the whole process, and its final execution boundary. Stage should retain the operational consequences of its own rules while Proposal owns how a submitted proposal proceeds through them. |
| Merge into Proposal | The same proposal, lifecycle, and details page currently have split explanations. Keep the configurable step separate and combine proposal continuity, coordination, and interaction. | Strongest fit for the current text and WORKFLOW's rule to keep a subject's behavior and lifecycle together. The cost is a longer Proposal page; section links preserve direct access without creating another product object. |
