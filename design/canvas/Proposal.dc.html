Proposal

Summary

Allocate 250,000 USDC and 400,000 MRD to the Ecosystem Grants Program to fund integrations, tooling and research during Q1 2027.

Motivation

Over the last two quarters Meridian has grown routed volume by 38% while the number of active contributors has stayed flat. This proposal is part of the community roadmap ratified in MGP-3 and has been discussed on the forum for two weeks.

Budget

250,000 USDC for integrations and tooling, 400,000 MRD for long-term research grants vesting over 12 months.

Actions

The proposal must pass before its actions can be executed.
